## Priority Issues
- [P0] The 32pt status line contradicts the opening replay (status() reads only state.phase). Fix: demo() in UiContext -> "WATCH — THE RECORD"; on skip, ticker "Skipped. Your chain starts at one." (clarify)
- [P1] A miss looks like a hit; lives hidden; "MISSED — REPLAYING" shown on the third miss; position vs length copy. Fix: light the expected pad during FAIL_HOLD, no flash on miss; "MISSED — 2 LIVES LEFT / LAST LIFE / OUT OF LIVES"; "missed link 3 of 5"; "Chain of 5 is gone." (clarify)
- [P1] Two records, one target, no payoff for the weekly beat; "beat it" about the player's own number. Fix: "took this week's best at N"; "yours at 5. Keep it."; header WEEK'S BEST n. (clarify)
- [P2] The product's voice is the smallest, faintest text; builder names rewrite every 0.42s during replay. Fix: ticker 20pt/0.85, stats 18; one composed "Built by …" line for the whole replay. (typeset)
- [P2] Fat-finger geometry: ~5px gutters, INVITE 1% above ROBOT at pad weight during input. Fix: pads 23%x44%, INVITE bottom 41%, INVITE only in choosing. (layout)
- [P3] Chain length told three ways. Drop CHAIN from header and "— chain is N". (distill)
